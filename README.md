### A few projects worth highlighting

**[The Fifth Continent](https://github.com/icarusfall/fifth-continent)**
A simple trading game set in a fictionalised Sussex, where you play a wool smuggler — an *owler*.
· [play it](https://fifth-continent.vercel.app)

**[Prisoner's Dilemma Tournament](https://github.com/icarusfall/prisoners-dilemma-tournament)**
An app for running iterated prisoner's dilemma tournaments and watching strategies fight it out.
· [try it](https://prisoners-dilemma-tournament-fronte.vercel.app)

**[Lego Sorter](https://github.com/icarusfall/lego-sorter)**
The beginnings of a machine for sorting and categorising a Lego collection.

**[Poetry Memoriser](https://github.com/icarusfall/poetry-memoriser)**
A very simple app to help memorise poetry.

**[Risk Training](https://github.com/icarusfall/risk-training)**
A training course in the statistics of estimating portfolio risk.

**[Ski Lift Records](https://github.com/icarusfall/ski-lift-records)**
A tracker and data explorer for weather and lift conditions across the larger European ski resorts.
